F-01

Comparing Two

比较两者

💡 概念 Concept

To compare two things, use the comparative: short adjectives add -er, long adjectives use more, and than introduces the other side of the comparison.比较两者用比较级：短形容词加 -er，长形容词用 more，than 引出比较的另一方。

📐 规则 Rules

短词 -er
tall → taller; fast → faster; big → bigger
高→更高；快→更快；大→更大
长词 more
careful → more careful; interesting → more interesting
仔细→更仔细；有趣→更有趣
引出比较对象
than: Tom is taller than me.
than：汤姆比我高。
程度修饰
much / a lot / a little + 比较级: much taller
much / a lot / a little + 比较级：高得多

✏️ 例句 Examples

My bag is heavier than yours.
我的包比你的重。
This movie is more interesting than that one.
这部电影比那部更有趣。
She is much more patient than her brother.
她比她哥哥有耐心得多。

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ He is more tall than me. → One comparative is enough: He is taller than me.❌ 不要重复比较级：He is taller than me.（他比我高。）
❌ This is more better. → Say: This is better.❌ better 已是比较级，前面不加 more：This is better.（这个更好。）
F-02

Superlatives

三者以上最高级

💡 概念 Concept

To rank three or more things, use the superlative: the + short adjective + -est, or the most + long adjective. Say which group you mean with in or of.三者以上排序用最高级：the + 短形容词 + -est，或 the most + 长形容词。用 in / of 说明范围。

📐 规则 Rules

短词 the -est
the tallest; the biggest; the happiest
最高的；最大的；最快乐的
长词 the most
the most careful; the most beautiful
最仔细的；最美丽的
范围 in / of
in + 地点/团体: in our class; of + 复数: of all
in + 地点/集体：在我们班；of + 复数：在所有之中
one of the...
one of the best players（最高级后接复数名词）
最优秀的球员之一（最高级后用复数名词）

✏️ 例句 Examples

Everest is the highest mountain in the world.
珠穆朗玛峰是世界上最高的山。
She is the most careful student in our class.
她是我们班最认真的学生。
It was one of the best days of my life.
那是我一生中最美好的日子之一。

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ She is tallest in our class. → Superlatives need the: She is the tallest in our class.❌ 最高级前面要加 the：She is the tallest in our class.（她是我们班最高的。）
❌ the most biggest → the biggest❌ biggest 已是最高级，不要加 most：the biggest（最大的）。
F-03

Irregulars & Spelling

不规则与拼写

💡 概念 Concept

A few very common adjectives are irregular: good → better → best, bad → worse → worst, much / many → more → most, little → less → least. Spelling also changes for short words: big → bigger, happy → happier, nice → nicer.少数常用形容词是不规则的：good → better → best（好）、bad → worse → worst（坏）、much / many → more → most（多）、little → less → least（少）。短词拼写也会变化：big → bigger、happy → happier、nice → nicer。

📐 规则 Rules

good / bad
good → better → best; bad → worse → worst
好→更好→最好；坏→更坏→最坏
多 / 少
much / many → more → most; little → less → least
多→更多→最多；少→更少→最少
双写
big → bigger → biggest; hot → hotter → hottest
大→更大→最大；热→更热→最热
y 与 e
happy → happier → happiest; nice → nicer → nicest
快乐→更快乐→最快乐；好→更好→最好

✏️ 例句 Examples

Today is better than yesterday.
今天比昨天好。
This is the worst weather we have had this week.
这是本周最糟糕的天气。
My little sister is the happiest girl I know.
我妹妹是我认识的最快乐的女孩。

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ My English is gooder than his. → good is irregular: better❌ good 的比较级是 better：My English is better than his.（我的英语比他好。）
❌ She is more happy now. → Short word: happier❌ happy 直接变 happier：She is happier now.（她现在更快乐。）
❌ This is the most worst day. → the worst❌ worst 已是最高级：This is the worst day.（这是最糟糕的一天。）
F-04

as ... as 同等比较

同等与不及比较

💡 概念 Concept

Use as + adjective + as to say two things are equal: as tall as. Use not as + adjective + as to say something is less: not as expensive as = cheaper than.用 as + 形容词 + as 表示两者相同：as tall as（一样高）。用 not as + 形容词 + as 表示“不如”：not as expensive as（不如……贵）= cheaper than（比……便宜）。

📐 规则 Rules

同等
as + 原级 + as: as tall as, as fast as
as + 形容词原级 + as：一样高、一样快
不如
not as + 原级 + as: not as cheap as
not as + 原级 + as：不如……便宜
副词也适用
as well as, as carefully as
副词也能这样用：一样好、一样仔细

✏️ 例句 Examples

He is as tall as his father.
他和他爸爸一样高。
This phone is not as expensive as that one.
这部手机不如那部贵。
She sings as well as her sister.
她唱得和她姐姐一样好。

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ She is as taller as me. → Use the base form: as tall as❌ as...as 之间用原级：She is as tall as me.（她和我一样高。）
❌ This book is not as cheap than that one. → not as cheap as❌ 否定同等用 not as...as：This book is not as cheap as that one.（这本书不如那本便宜。）
