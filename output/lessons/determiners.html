\n 08-01\n

Articles

\n 冠词\n
\n

💡 概念 Concept

a/an = one, non-specific, first mention (a before consonants, an before vowels); the = specific or known; zero article = general plural or uncountable.a/an 表【一个】，不特指，首次提及（辅音前用 a，元音前用 an）；the 表特指或已知；零冠词用于泛指复数或不可数。
\n

📐 规则 Rules

a/an
I saw a dog. / an apple; an hour (silent h).
我见到一只狗。/ 一个苹果；an hour（h 不发音）。
the
The dog I saw was black. (specific)
我见到的那只狗是黑色的。（特指）
零冠词
Dogs are friendly. / Water is important. (general)
狗很友好。/ 水很重要。（泛指）
\n

✏️ 例句 Examples

She bought a car last week.
她上周买了一辆车。
The car is parked outside.
那辆车停在门外。
Children love ice cream.
孩子们喜欢冰淇淋。
\n

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ an university → a university (yoo sound).❌ university 发 /juː/，用 a。
❌ The dogs are friendly (general) → zero article: Dogs are friendly.❌ 泛指复数不加 the。

\n 08-02\n

Definite vs Indefinite

\n 定指与不定指\n
\n

💡 概念 Concept

Use the when both speaker and listener know which one (mentioned before, unique, or clear from context). Use a/an for new or one-of-many.说话双方都知道是哪一个（前面提过、独一无二或语境明确）时用 the；新提到或众多之一用 a/an。
\n

📐 规则 Rules

提过
I saw a bird. The bird was singing.
我看到一只鸟。那只鸟在唱歌。
独一无二
the sun, the moon, the president
the sun（太阳）、the moon（月亮）
语境明确
Please close the door. (we know which door)
请关门。（知道是哪扇门）
\n

✏️ 例句 Examples

There is a book on the desk. The book is mine.
桌上有一本书。那本书是我的。
She is the manager of this store.
她是这家店的经理。
I need a pen. Do you have one?
我需要一支笔。你有吗？
\n

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ I saw the bird (first mention) → a bird.❌ 首次提及用 a。
❌ the first mention of a non-specific thing → a/an.❌ 不特指时用 a/an。
\n

\n 08-03\n

Quantifiers

\n 量词\n
\n

💡 概念 Concept

some = positive/offer; any = negative/question; much/little = uncountable; many/few = countable; a few = some (positive), few = almost none.some 用于肯定/提议；any 用于否定/疑问；much/little 修饰不可数；many/few 修饰可数；a few 表示【有一些】（肯定），few 表示【几乎没有】。
\n

📐 规则 Rules

可数
many books, a few friends, few chances
many books（很多书）、a few friends（几个朋友）、few chances（几乎没有机会）
不可数
much time, a little sugar, little water
much time（很多时间）、a little sugar（一点糖）、little water（几乎没有水）
some/any
I have some money. Do you have any? No, I don\'t have any.
我有些钱。你有吗？不，我没有。
\n

✏️ 例句 Examples

Would you like some tea?
要来点茶吗？
There aren\'t many seats left.
剩下的座位不多了。
She has a few good ideas.
她有一些好主意。
\n

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ many water → much water (uncountable).❌ 不可数用 much。
❌ I don\'t have some → any in negatives: I don\'t have any.❌ 否定句用 any。

\n 08-04\n

Demonstratives

\n 指示词\n
\n

💡 概念 Concept

this/these = near; that/those = far. They point at people or things and agree in number.this/these 表近；that/those 表远。指示词在单复数上要一致。
\n

📐 规则 Rules

近
this book (singular), these books (plural)
this book（这本书）、these books（这些书）
远
that house, those houses
that house（那栋房子）、those houses（那些房子）
\n

✏️ 例句 Examples

This is my friend Tom.
这是我的朋友汤姆。
Those shoes are expensive.
那些鞋很贵。
Look at that bird in the tree.
看树上的那只鸟。
\n

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ these book → these books (plural).❌ these 后接复数。
❌ this books → this book.❌ this 后接单数。
\n

\n 08-05\n

Possessives

\n 所有格\n
\n

💡 概念 Concept

Possessive determiners: my, your, his, her, its, our, their. They always come before a noun and agree with the owner, not the thing.所有格限定词：my、your、his、her、its、our、their。它们修饰名词，与所有者一致，而非与事物一致。
\n

📐 规则 Rules

形式
my book, your phone, his car, her bag, its tail, our home, their toys
my book（我的书）、your phone（你的手机）、his car（他的车）……
不随事物
She has a brother. Her brother is tall. (her matches she)
her 与所有者 she 一致。
\n

✏️ 例句 Examples

Is this your umbrella?
这是你的伞吗？
The dog wagged its tail.
狗摇了摇尾巴。
Their house is near the river.
他们的房子在河边。
\n

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ its vs it\'s: its = possessive; it\'s = it is.❌ its（它的）与 it\'s（它是）别混淆。
❌ her brother and his sister — match the owner, not the noun.❌ 所有格与所有者一致。
\n

\n 08-06\n

Generic Reference

\n 类指\n
\n

💡 概念 Concept

Talk about a whole class with: zero article + plural (Dogs are loyal), the + singular (The lion is a big cat), or a/an + singular (A teacher helps students).谈论整个类别：零冠词 + 复数（Dogs are loyal）、the + 单数（The lion...）、或 a/an + 单数（A teacher...）。
\n

📐 规则 Rules

复数类指
Cats are independent.
猫是独立的。（泛指所有猫）
the + 单数
The smartphone changed communication.
智能手机改变了沟通方式。
a/an + 单数
A doctor saves lives.
医生救死扶伤。
\n

✏️ 例句 Examples

Elephants are intelligent animals.
大象是聪明的动物。
The bicycle is a green way to travel.
自行车是绿色出行方式。
A good book is a good friend.
好书如好友。
\n

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ The elephants are intelligent (general) → zero article: Elephants are...❌ 泛指用零冠词。
❌ Mixing plural generic with singular verb.❌ 类指单复数要一致。

\n 08-07\n

Determiner Contrast

\n 限定词对比\n
\n

💡 概念 Concept

Choose the determiner by what you mean: known (the/this), unknown (a/an/some/any), quantity (many/much/few/little), ownership (my/your/his).按含义选限定词：已知（the/this）、未知（a/an/some/any）、数量（many/much/few/little）、所属（my/your/his）。
\n

📐 规则 Rules

已知性
the/this for known; a/an/some for new.
已知用 the/this；新信息用 a/an/some。
数量
countable: many/few; uncountable: much/little.
可数：many/few；不可数：much/little。
\n

✏️ 例句 Examples

I bought some apples. The apples are in the fridge.
我买了些苹果。苹果在冰箱里。
How much time do we have?
我们还有多少时间？
There are few people in the park today.
今天公园里人很少。
\n

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ How many time → how much time (uncountable).❌ time 不可数，用 how much。
❌ few vs a few: few = almost none; a few = some.❌ few 几乎为零；a few 有一些。

\n 08-08\n

Production and Editing

\n 综合运用与改错\n
\n

💡 概念 Concept

Edit texts by asking about each noun: Is it specific or general? Countable or uncountable? Singular or plural? Then choose the determiner.改错时对每个名词提问：特指还是泛指？可数还是不可数？单数还是复数？再选限定词。
\n

📐 规则 Rules

四问
specific? countable? plural? known? → the/a/an/zero/quantifier.
特指？可数？复数？已知？→ 决定冠词与量词。
首尾一致
The determiner must agree with the noun number.
限定词与名词单复数一致。
\n

✏️ 例句 Examples

❌ I have few questions to ask. (few = almost none) → I have a few questions to ask.
❌ 想说「有几个问题」用 a few。
❌ She gave me an useful advice → She gave me useful advice. (advice uncountable, no article)
❌ advice 不可数，不用 a/an。
✔ The teacher gave the students some useful advice.
✔ 老师给学生们一些有用的建议。
\n

⚠️ 常见错误 Common Mistakes

❌ informations → information (uncountable, no plural).❌ information 不可数。
❌ a equipment → equipment (uncountable): some equipment.❌ equipment 不可数。
